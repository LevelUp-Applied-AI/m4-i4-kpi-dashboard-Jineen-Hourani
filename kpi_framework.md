# KPI Framework — Amman Digital Market

Define 5 KPIs for the Amman Digital Market. At least 2 must be time-based and 1 must be cohort-based.

---

## KPI 1 (Time-based)

- **Name:** Monthly Total Revenue
- **Definition:** The total gross sales generated by the market on a monthly basis.
- **Formula:** `SUM(quantity * unit_price)` grouped by Month.
- **Data Source:** `orders`, `order_items`, `products` (columns: `order_date`, `quantity`, `unit_price`).
- **Baseline Value:** 2,705.64 JOD (Average monthly revenue across the period).
- **Interpretation:** Revenue shows significant peaks in March and June 2025 (exceeding 5,000 JOD), suggesting successful seasonal campaigns or high-value bulk purchases during these months.

---

## KPI 2 (Time-based)

- **Name:** Monthly Order Volume
- **Definition:** The total number of unique orders placed by customers each month.
- **Formula:** `COUNT(DISTINCT order_id)` grouped by Month.
- **Data Source:** `orders` (column: `order_id`, `order_date`).
- **Baseline Value:** 24.6 Orders/Month (Average).
- **Interpretation:** Order volume is generally stable at ~23 orders/month, but spiked to 47 orders in June 2025, perfectly correlating with the revenue spike and indicating an increase in customer traffic.

---

## KPI 3 (Cohort-based)

- **Name:** Revenue by City
- **Definition:** Total revenue segmented by the customer's registered city to identify geographic hotspots.
- **Formula:** `SUM(line_total)` grouped by `city`.
- **Data Source:** `customers`, `order_items` (columns: `city`, `line_total`).
- **Baseline Value:** Amman (15,719.0 JOD), Irbid (7,250.5 JOD).
- **Interpretation:** Amman is the primary market driver, contributing more than double the revenue of the next closest city (Irbid). This justifies focusing marketing spend on the capital.

---

## KPI 4 (Efficiency)

- **Name:** Average Order Value (AOV)
- **Definition:** The average amount of money spent by a customer per transaction.
- **Formula:** `Total Revenue / Total Unique Orders`.
- **Data Source:** Calculated from consolidated data in `orders` and `order_items`.
- **Baseline Value:** 109.94 JOD.
- **Interpretation:** An AOV of ~110 JOD suggests that customers are purchasing multiple items or high-ticket electronics/books per order, indicating healthy basket sizes.

---

## KPI 5 (Product Performance)

- **Name:** Revenue by Product Category
- **Definition:** Total sales value generated by each product category to identify top-performing sectors.
- **Formula:** `SUM(line_total)` grouped by `category`.
- **Data Source:** `products`, `order_items` (columns: `category`, `line_total`).
- **Baseline Value:** Books (11,274.0 JOD) and Electronics (11,005.0 JOD).
- **Interpretation:** Books and Electronics are the "powerhouse" categories. Despite being a digital market, physical/knowledge goods (Books) are slightly outperforming high-tech items.


---

### Statistical Validation for KPI 5: Product Performance

- **The Hypothesis:**
    - **H0 (Null Hypothesis):** There is no significant difference in the average transaction value across different product categories.
    - **H1 (Alternative Hypothesis):** At least one product category has a significantly different average transaction value compared to others.
- **The Test Used:** One-Way ANOVA.
    - *Why:* It compares the means of more than two independent groups.
- **Test Results:**
    - **Test Statistic (F):** 60.379
    - **P-value:** 1.628e-55
- **Effect Size:** - **Type:** Eta-squared
    - **Value:** 0.219
    - **Magnitude:** Large
- **Plain-language Interpretation:** We reject the Null Hypothesis. Category significantly influences spending.
