# Executive Summary — Amman Digital Market Analytics

## Top Findings

<!-- List 3-5 key findings from your analysis. Each finding should be one sentence
     that a non-technical stakeholder can understand. Support each with a specific
     number or statistic. -->

1. **Market Growth:** Monthly revenue reached a record peak of **5,086.50 JOD** in June 2025, showing strong seasonal growth.
2. **Geographic Dominance:** Amman is the primary revenue driver, contributing **15,719 JOD**, which is more than double the revenue of the next largest city, Irbid.
3. **Product Performance:** Books and Electronics are the most popular categories, together accounting for nearly **45%** of total revenue.
4. **Statistical Confirmation:** We have confirmed with high confidence (p < 0.001) that the product category significantly influences customer spending patterns.

## Recommendations

<!-- Based on your findings, what actions should the business take?
     Each recommendation should be specific, actionable, and tied to a finding above. -->

1. **Focused Marketing:** Allocate 60% of the digital advertising budget specifically to the **Amman** region to capitalize on the existing high demand.
2. **Inventory Optimization:** Increase stock levels for **Books and Electronics** before the anticipated seasonal spikes in March and June, based on the 2025 data trends.
3. **Category-Specific Promotions:** Since spending varies significantly by category, launch high-value "Bundle Deals" for **Electronics** to further increase the Average Order Value (AOV), which currently stands at **109.94 JOD**.
